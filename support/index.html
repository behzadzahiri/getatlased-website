Support

How can we help?

Answers to common questions. Anything else, just email us.

How does Atlased find my trips?

Tap “Find my trips” and allow Photos. Atlased reads only each photo's place and date, on your iPhone, and groups them into journeys. Trips with clear evidence are added for you. Anything uncertain waits in Build your journal, where you can review it before it is added.

A country or city is missing or wrong.

Open the journey and tap Edit to add, rename or remove a stop. For photos Atlased couldn't place, open Settings → Build your journal → Photos and tap “Photos without a place name” to name them yourself. If something is consistently wrong, email us a screenshot.

Why does Atlased ask about my home?

Knowing where you live lets Atlased tell trips from everyday life, so each journey starts and ends in the right place. Your home stays private on your iPhone and is never shown on shared pictures. You can confirm, change or remove homes in Build your journal.

How do I add flights and hotels?

On Journeys, tap “Add from calendar”, or open Build your journal → Calendar. Atlased reads flight and hotel events from the calendars you choose, including Google calendars added to your iPhone, and links them to your trips. If a flight looks wrong, open the journey and tap Edit.

A flight in my journal was someone else's.

Open the journey and tap “Not my flight” under Flights. Choose the flights, add the traveler's name if you like, then tap Review changes and Move to Others. Those flights leave your travel totals and stay in your flight journal under Others. To bring one back, open Recap → View flight journal → Others, tap the flight and choose “Mark as mine”.

Where is my wishlist?

Open Atlas and tap the bookmark button at the top right. Tap the plus button there to find a place to save.

Is my journal backed up?

Atlased keeps automatic backups on your iPhone, which you can see and restore in Settings → Journal health. Your iPhone's own backup can include your journal too. You can also export a backup file from Settings.

How do I restore or manage Atlased Pro?

Pro belongs to your Apple Account. To restore it, open Settings in Atlased, tap Atlased Pro, then Restore purchases. To change or cancel a subscription, go to iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

How do I export my itinerary to Excel?

With Atlased Pro, open Settings → Travel reports and choose a year or All time. Tap “Export Excel itinerary”, then share the file or save it to Files. The spreadsheet lists each trip's places, visit dates, hotel stays and flights. Notes stay in your journal. To leave journey titles out, open the options menu and turn off “Include journey titles”.

How do I see trip anniversary notifications on my Lock Screen?

Turn on Trip anniversaries in Atlased Settings and allow notifications when iOS asks. If “Show on Lock Screen” appears, tap it to open the notification controls in iPhone Settings, then turn on Lock Screen alerts. If you selected only Notification Center, look for reminders there.

Does Atlased upload my photos?

Atlased builds your journal on your iPhone. By default, it sends Apple only photo coordinates outside your home areas to find missing place names. To turn this off, open Settings → Build your journal → Photos and switch off “Look up places with Apple”. Your iPhone can also fetch photos from your own iCloud Photos library when you open them.
